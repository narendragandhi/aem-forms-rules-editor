# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and professional. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies to project spaces and interactions representing the project.
